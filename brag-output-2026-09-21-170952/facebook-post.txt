Not sure if this is useful to anyone else, but sharing anyway.

I was applying for my Masters and completely lost track of everything in a
spreadsheet — which uni still needed which document, whether a deadline was
uni-assist's or the university's, which version of my motivation letter went
where. I'd open the sheet after two weeks and half of it made no sense to me
anymore.

So I ended up building something for myself. Every application is its own
record with its deadline, documents, portal link and one next step, so you can
actually pick it back up later. You can paste a programme or job link and it
fills in the details for you. It works for job applications too — I needed
that part sooner than I expected.

Free, and you can import your spreadsheet if you already have one.

applume.app

If you try it and something's annoying or missing, just tell me.
